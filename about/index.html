Our Story

Built for neighbors, by neighbors

Junes Junk Removal started because of a frustration most people around here know well: you’ve got stuff that needs to go, and getting it hauled away is either too expensive, too unreliable, or too complicated. We wanted to change that — not just for one job, but consistently, for anyone who called us.

We’re a local junk removal company based in New Bedford, MA. We serve our own neighbors — the homeowners cleaning out before a sale, the landlords turning over a unit, the families managing an estate after a loss, the contractors who need debris off a job site before the next crew shows up. These are real situations with real urgency, and we take that seriously.

Over the past 5+ years, we’ve built a reputation on the South Coast for showing up on time, doing what we said we’d do, and leaving every property cleaner than we found it. We’re not the biggest hauling company out there, but we work hard to be the most reliable one you’ll find in this area. That’s what keeps people calling us back — and sending their family and friends our way.

Our Mission

Simple, honest, stress-free

To make junk removal in New Bedford, MA and the surrounding South Coast simple, honest, and stress-free. That means clear pricing before we start, a crew that actually shows up when they say they will, and a job that’s done right the first time. We’re building a local junk removal company that people can count on — not just for one job, but every time they need us.
