Responsible E-Waste Recycling for Your Home & Business

In Massachusetts, electronics are banned from regular trash disposal because they contain heavy metals and hazardous materials that can harm the environment. This makes getting rid of old TVs, computers, and monitors a challenge for many residents. June’s Junk Removal offers a simple, professional solution for electronic disposal in New Bedford and throughout the South Coast.

We handle the entire process for you. Our crew will come into your home or office, safely remove the items from wherever they’re located, and load them into our truck. You don’t have to worry about the heavy lifting of old tube TVs or the logistics of getting equipment to a recycling center. We serve homeowners clearing out a single device and businesses looking to liquidate entire departments worth of IT equipment.

Our commitment to the environment means we don’t just dump your electronics. We take all collected e-waste to specialized recycling facilities that are equipped to properly dismantle and process the materials. This ensures that valuable components are recovered and hazardous substances are managed safely. From scheduling to final haul-away, we make responsible electronic disposal fast and effortless.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our computer and electronics disposal service.

What electronics do you remove and dispose of?

Desktops, laptops and tablets, monitors and screens, TVs of all sizes including old tube sets, printers and copiers, networking gear, peripherals, phones, stereos and speakers, and miscellaneous cables and chargers.

Are electronics properly recycled or just sent to landfill?

Properly recycled. Massachusetts bans electronics from regular trash, so we take all collected e-waste to specialized facilities equipped to dismantle and process it safely.

Is data on my devices secure when you dispose of them?

We recommend wiping or removing drives before pickup if the device holds sensitive data. We transport items directly to recycling facilities and do not resell or redistribute devices.

Do you handle large quantities of electronics for businesses?

Yes. We handle office refreshes, department liquidations, school equipment disposal, and recurring pickups for IT departments.

Can I combine electronics disposal with a larger junk removal job?

Absolutely — it’s common. We’ll separate the e-waste from the rest of the load and route each stream to the right facility.
