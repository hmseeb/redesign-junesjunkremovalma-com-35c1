Reclaim Your Outdoor Space

Whether it’s from storm damage, overgrown vegetation, or seasonal maintenance, yard debris can quickly take over your property. Piles of branches and brush aren’t just an eyesore — they can also become home to pests and prevent you from fully enjoying your yard. June’s Junk Removal offers full-service yard debris removal throughout New Bedford and the South Coast, providing a fast and easy way to clear your outdoor space.

Our crew handles the heavy and often awkward labor of gathering, loading, and hauling away yard waste. From large fallen limbs after a storm to piles of brush and clippings from a weekend of gardening, we show up prepared with the right equipment and experience to get the job done quickly. We serve homeowners, landlords, and property managers who want professional results without the physical strain.

Responsible disposal is important to us, especially when it comes to organic waste. Whenever possible, we take yard debris to composting or recycling facilities that can turn old branches and leaves into something useful. From your first call to the final sweep-up, our goal is to provide a stress-free experience that leaves your yard looking its best.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our yard debris removal service in New Bedford and the South Coast.

What yard debris do you remove?

Branches and limbs, brush and shrubs, bagged leaves and grass, overgrown vines, garden waste, old mulch and landscape fabric, excess soil, wooden pallets, old fencing, and storm debris.

Does yard debris need to be bagged first?

No. Loose piles are fine — we gather and load it ourselves. Bagging smaller material just speeds loading up, which can help on price.

Can you clear heavily overgrown areas?

Yes. Overgrown lots, invasive vines, and neglected yards are common jobs for us. Send a photo and we’ll scope it accurately.

Do you remove yard debris after a storm?

Yes, and we try to respond quickly. Downed limbs and wind-scattered debris are routine work across the South Coast.

Can you haul large tree branches and logs?

We handle large limbs and cut logs. We’re a hauling crew rather than a tree service, so felling standing trees isn’t something we do — but we’ll clear what’s already down.
