Common Items We Remove

If you’re not sure whether we take something, just call or text — we’ll let you know. We remove most non-hazardous items. Here’s a quick look at what we handle every day:

  • Furniture — sofas, chairs, tables, dressers, bed frames
  • Mattresses & box springs
  • Appliances — refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens, microwaves
  • Electronics — TVs, computers, monitors, printers
  • Construction debris — wood, drywall, tile, concrete, metal
  • Yard waste — branches, brush piles, old fencing, landscaping materials
  • Household junk — boxes, clutter, stored items, general property waste
  • Office equipment — desks, chairs, filing cabinets, cubicle parts
We cannot remove hazardous materials such as asbestos, fuels, or regulated chemicals. If you’re unsure, ask us and we’ll point you in the right direction.
