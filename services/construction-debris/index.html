Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our construction debris removal service in New Bedford and the South Coast.

What types of construction debris do you remove?

Lumber and wood scraps, drywall and plaster, tile and backer board, carpet and padding, roofing materials, insulation, small loads of concrete and masonry, old windows and doors, piping and fixtures, and general demolition waste.

Do you remove debris during an ongoing renovation or only after?

Either. We can clear debris mid-project to keep the site safe and workable, or do a single haul-away once the work is finished.

Can you haul away mixed loads of renovation waste?

Yes. Mixed loads are what we do most — lumber, drywall, tile, fixtures, and general debris all in one trip. No sorting needed on your end.

Do you handle heavy materials like concrete or tile?

Yes, in small loads. Brick, block, broken concrete, and tile are dense, so we’ll confirm the volume up front and quote it accordingly.

Can you provide ongoing debris removal for a larger project?

We can. For longer projects, many contractors pair recurring pickups with a dump trailer rental on site.

How quickly can you schedule construction debris pickup?

Often same-day or next-day. We know construction timelines are tight, so call or text 774-400-8888 and we’ll tell you the soonest window.

Do you work with contractors directly?

Yes. We work regularly with general contractors, kitchen and bath remodelers, roofers, property managers, and flippers across the South Coast.
