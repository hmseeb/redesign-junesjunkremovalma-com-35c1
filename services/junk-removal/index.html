Full-Service Junk Removal — We Handle Everything

When clutter takes over a garage, basement, or rental property, the problem isn’t just the mess — it’s the time, labor, and logistics of dealing with it. June’s Junk Removal offers full-service junk hauling for homes and businesses throughout New Bedford and the surrounding South Coast communities. Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our junk removal service in New Bedford and the South Coast.

What kinds of junk do you remove?

We take most non-hazardous items — furniture, mattresses and box springs, appliances, electronics, construction debris, yard waste, household clutter, office equipment, and full storage-unit or basement contents. We cannot take hazardous materials such as asbestos, fuels, or regulated chemicals. If you’re unsure about an item, call or text 774-400-8888 and we’ll confirm.

Do I need to move the items outside first?

No. This is full-service junk removal — we come to wherever the items are and carry them out ourselves. If it’s easier for you to set things at the curb, we also offer curbside pickup.

Do you handle heavy lifting?

Yes. Our crew does all the lifting, carrying, and loading, from any room, any floor, or anywhere on the property. You don’t touch a thing.

Can you remove junk from basements, attics, and garages?

Absolutely. Basements, attics, and garages are some of our most common jobs. We’re experienced with narrow stairs, pull-down ladders, and tight storage spaces.

Do you offer residential and commercial junk removal?

Both. We work with homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers, real estate agents, contractors, and business owners throughout Massachusetts and Rhode Island.

What happens to my items after removal?

We donate usable items to local organizations and recycle metals, electronics, and cardboard whenever possible. Whatever can’t be donated or recycled is disposed of at licensed facilities in compliance with local regulations.

Do I need to be on-site during removal?

Not always. If the items are accessible and we’ve agreed on the scope and price ahead of time, we can complete many jobs without you being there. Just let us know when you book.

How do I schedule a junk removal pickup?

Call or text 774-400-8888, or send the estimate form on this page. Photos of the items get you the fastest quote. We can often schedule same-day or next-day pickups.
