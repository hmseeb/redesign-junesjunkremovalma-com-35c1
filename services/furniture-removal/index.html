Hassle-Free Furniture Hauling & Disposal

Moving furniture is one of the most physically demanding parts of any cleanout or renovation. Large pieces like sectionals, armoires, and solid wood desks are not only heavy but often awkward to maneuver through tight hallways and staircases. At June’s Junk Removal, we specialize in room-to-room furniture removal, meaning we pick up your unwanted items exactly where they are.

Our professional crew arrives with the right equipment and experience to remove items safely without damaging your walls or floors. Whether you’re upgrading a single room or clearing out an entire estate, we handle every piece with respect. We save you the time and risk of injury associated with trying to lift and haul heavy furniture yourself.

We are committed to responsible disposal. If your furniture is still in good, usable condition, we prioritize donating it to local organizations to help families in need across the South Coast. Items that cannot be donated are recycled or disposed of according to local environmental regulations. You get your space back, and your old furniture is handled with care.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our furniture removal service.

What types of furniture do you remove?

Sofas and sectionals, beds and bed frames, dressers and armoires, dining sets, recliners, desks, bookshelves, entertainment centers, office furniture, and outdoor or patio furniture — individual pieces or complete room sets.

Do you disassemble furniture before removal?

Yes, when it’s needed. Our crew comes equipped to break down bed frames, sectionals, shelving, and cubicles so pieces can be carried out safely through tight hallways and stairwells.

Can you remove furniture from upstairs rooms or tight spaces?

Yes. We offer room-to-room service, so we pick up items exactly where they are — third-floor apartments, narrow staircases, and basements included. You don’t need to move anything first.

Do you donate furniture that’s still in good condition?

We do. Usable furniture is prioritized for donation to local South Coast organizations. What can’t be donated is recycled or disposed of according to local environmental regulations.

Do I need to be home during the furniture removal?

Not necessarily. If the pieces are accessible and the scope and price are agreed in advance, we can complete the pickup without you on-site. Just let us know when you book.

Do you handle both residential and commercial furniture removal?

Yes — homes, apartments, rentals, offices, and retail spaces. We’re licensed and insured for residential and commercial work in Massachusetts and Rhode Island.

How do I schedule a furniture pickup?

Call or text 774-400-8888 or send the form on this page. A photo of the furniture and your address gets you the fastest quote, and we often have same-day or next-day availability.
