Professional Basement Clearing for Your South Coast Home

Basements are notorious for accumulating items that “might be useful someday.” Over the years, this leads to a space filled with old furniture, dusty boxes, and forgotten belongings. June’s Junk Removal specializes in basement cleanouts across New Bedford and the South Coast, providing the physical labor and expertise needed to clear out even the most cluttered underground spaces.

Navigating steep, narrow basement stairs with heavy items is both difficult and dangerous. Our experienced crew comes prepared with the right equipment and techniques to safely move bulky furniture, old appliances, and heavy bins out of your basement and into our truck. We handle everything from single-room clearouts to full basement decluttering, working efficiently to give you back your square footage.

Beyond just hauling away junk, we focus on a stress-free experience. We sort through items as we go, setting aside recyclables and pieces in good condition for local donation centers. Once the basement is clear, we sweep the entire area, leaving it clean and ready for your next project — whether that’s finishing the space for a home gym or simply enjoying an organized storage area.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our basement cleanout service in New Bedford and the South Coast.

What types of items do you remove from basements?

Old furniture, boxes and storage bins, lumber and renovation scraps, exercise equipment, holiday decorations, old electronics, bags of clothing and linens, power tools, bicycles, and general clutter.

Do you remove furniture and appliances from the basement?

Yes. Couches, dressers, freezers, washers, dryers, and water heaters are all common basement removals for us.

How do you handle items in a cluttered or tight basement?

We work in stages, clearing a path first so bulky items can be moved safely. Our crew is used to low ceilings, support posts, and packed storage areas.

Can you remove heavy or bulky items up the stairs?

That’s our specialty. Steep, narrow basement stairs are the main reason people call us — we bring the right equipment and techniques to do it safely.

Do I need to sort or organize anything before the cleanout?

No. We sort as we go, setting aside recyclables and donation-worthy pieces. Just tell us anything you want kept before we start.

Do you clean up after removing everything?

Yes. We sweep the entire area before we leave, so you get a clean, usable space — not a dusty one.
