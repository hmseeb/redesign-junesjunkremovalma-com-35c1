Professional Mattress & Bedding Disposal

Getting rid of an old mattress is often the most frustrating part of upgrading your bedroom. They are awkward to carry, don’t fit in most vehicles, and many local trash services won’t take them without special arrangements. At June’s Junk Removal, we specialize in fast, full-service mattress removal that takes the stress out of the process.

Our team handles everything from single twin mattresses to large king-size sets and heavy memory foam beds. We come right into your home, navigate the stairs and tight hallways, and carry the mattress out for you. You don’t have to lift a finger or worry about how to get it to the curb. We work with homeowners, renters, and property managers throughout New Bedford and the South Coast.

We are committed to responsible disposal practices. Mattresses contain materials like steel, wood, and foam that can often be recycled. We work with local facilities to ensure that your old bed is disposed of according to local regulations, keeping as much as possible out of landfills. When you choose June’s, you’re choosing a local team that cares about our community.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our mattress removal and disposal service in New Bedford.

Do you take mattresses in any condition?

Yes. Old, stained, torn, or sagging — condition doesn’t matter. We remove twin through California king, innerspring, pillow top, and memory foam.

Do mattresses need to be wrapped or bagged?

No. You don’t need to wrap or bag anything. We handle the mattress exactly as it is and carry it out ourselves.

Can you remove a mattress from an upstairs bedroom?

Yes. Our crew comes inside, navigates stairs and tight hallways, and carries the mattress out. You never have to drag it to the curb.

Do you remove box springs as well?

We do — on their own or bundled with the mattress in a single visit. See our box spring removal page for details.

How are mattresses disposed of?

Mattresses contain steel, wood, and foam that can often be recycled, so we work with local facilities and follow local regulations to keep as much as possible out of landfills.

How quickly can you schedule mattress pickup?

Same-day or next-day service is often available. Call or text 774-400-8888 and we’ll tell you what’s open.
