Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about junk removal in New Bedford, MA and the surrounding area.

How do I schedule junk removal in New Bedford, MA?

Call or text 774-400-8888, or send the estimate form on this page. Describe what you have — photos get you the fastest quote — and we’ll give you an upfront price and a pickup window. Same-day and next-day appointments are often available.

How much does junk removal cost in New Bedford?

Pricing is based on volume and labour, and you always get a clear price before any work begins — no fuel surcharges or disposal fees added at the end. Common items start at $80 for bulky items and $30 each for electronics, CFC/CRT units, and tires. Text a photo to 774-400-8888 for the fastest estimate.

What items do you remove in New Bedford, MA?

We take most non-hazardous items: furniture, mattresses and box springs, appliances, electronics, construction debris, yard waste, and general household junk — one piece or a full truckload. We cannot remove hazardous materials such as asbestos, fuels, or regulated chemicals.

Do you serve areas outside of New Bedford?

Yes. We’re based in New Bedford and serve 42+ communities across the South Coast, Plymouth County, Cape Cod, the Fall River and Taunton area, and the Rhode Island East Bay. See our service areas page for the full list.

How quickly can you pick up junk in New Bedford?

We can often accommodate same-day and next-day pickups, especially close to home on the South Coast. Call or text 774-400-8888 and we’ll tell you the soonest window we have.

Are you licensed and insured for junk removal in Massachusetts?

Yes. June’s Junk Removal is fully licensed and insured for residential and commercial work throughout Massachusetts and Rhode Island, and we maintain the required permits for waste hauling and disposal.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Not always. If the items are accessible — at the curb, in a driveway, or in an unlocked garage — and we’ve agreed on scope and price in advance, we can complete the job without you being there.
